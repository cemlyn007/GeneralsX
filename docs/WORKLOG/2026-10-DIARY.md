### PLAN-023 Phase 8, Stage RR3: Per-Engine W3DDevice State, Logic-Frame Draw Clock and Per-Engine RNGs
- **Context**: after RR2b every render engine owned its device, its `DX8Wrapper`, `WW3D` and `ShaderClass` state and WW3D2's subsystems, but W3DDevice's draw state (the shader manager's shader and filter objects, the shadow buffers, terrain, view and display statics) was still one per process, a few timed effects read the wall clock, and the snow table and camera shake drew from the C library's `rand()`. rlgenerals' host could run only one renderer.
- **Changes**:
  - `W3DShaderManager`: with `RTS_ENGINE_CONTEXT` its class statics, shader and filter tables and the screen filters' fade and zoom statics are fields of `W3DShaderManagerData`, and the shader and filter objects and their lists are members of `W3DShaderManagerState`, one `PER_ENGINE_STATIC` value-initialised like the statics; the header's inline accessors are out of line with the context.
  - Shadows: the volumetric and projected shadow managers own their dynamic buffers, cursors, last vertex buffer and culling box, `W3DShadowManager` the camera frustum and light position; the scratch is local.
  - Members of their owners: the height map's extra-blend size, the flat height map's logged counts, `W3DView`'s follow factor, `W3DDisplay::draw`'s frame-skip counter and render flag, the water tracks' and shroud's clocks, `SmudgeSet`'s free list (now the manager's); the height map's visible cell box, `draw`'s letterbox time and the infantry light cap are locals.
  - `W3DDrawClock.h`: the letterbox fade, the water sky and update times, the water tracks and the shroud's fog interpolation read WW3D's sync time (logic frames) in render-headless mode, the wall clock otherwise.
  - Per-engine `Random3Class` generators for the snow table, `WWMath::Random_Float` and the shatter pattern choice.
  - RR2b review follow-ups: constexpr reverse format tables, queued loader tasks freed, `Request_Thumbnail`'s upstream order, `OwnedByWW3D` initialised, the defaults guard rolls back to the prior protection, the loader's lock dropping gated on `_UNIX`, per-function bindings in `pointgr` and `sortingrenderer`.
