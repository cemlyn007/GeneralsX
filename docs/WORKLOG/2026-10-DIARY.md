### Compare `AIAttackState`'s Locked Weapon by Slot, Not by Address
- **Context**: rlgenerals' AI-vs-AI replays sometimes desynced on playback, and the same game recorded twice did not always play out the same. For example, USA hard vs USA hard on Tournament Desert with the shroud on, seed 106, split at frame 25802 on one Ranger. `AIAttackState` kept the `Weapon*` that was current and locked when the attack began, and failed the attack as soon as `getCurrentWeapon()` returned a different pointer. `WeaponSet::updateWeaponSet` deletes and reallocates every `Weapon` when the weapon set changes, and a lock the new set shares across sets survives that. The comparison then tested a freed address against a new one. With the game memory pools off (`RTS_GAMEMEMORY_ENABLE=OFF`, as in rlgenerals' `libgeneralsx.so`), glibc malloc decides whether the new `Weapon` lands at the old address. That depends on the state of the heap (whether that size's tcache bin is full when the old `Weapon` is freed), not on the simulation, so the same unit kept or dropped its attack from run to run.
- **Changes** (Zero Hour and Generals, `AIStateMachine.h` and `AIStates.cpp`): `AIAttackState` now records whether it entered with a locked weapon (`m_hasLockedWeaponOnEnter`) and that weapon's slot (`m_lockedWeaponSlotOnEnter`, from `getCurrentWeapon(&slot)`), in `onEnter` and `loadPostProcess`. `update` fails the attack when the unit entered locked and now has no current weapon or a different current slot. Neither field is saved by `xfer` or included in the CRC, as before.
- **Behaviour**: `updateWeaponSet` frees and reallocates one slot at a time. With the pools on, or with room in the tcache bin, the new `Weapon` in the same slot gets the old address back. Comparing slots therefore keeps that common case and removes the dependence on the allocator.
- **Validation** (rlgenerals, Zero Hour, USA hard vs USA hard, shroud on):
  - Seeds 31, 100 to 110, 134 and 184 were each recorded twice plainly and twice with a probe that fills and empties every glibc tcache bin after each frame. All 4 recordings of each seed give the same CRC every 100 frames, and each recording plays back with and without the probe with no CRC mismatch.
  - Before the fix, seed 106's recordings split at frame 21000 or 25900, and its replays desynced in most runs. Seeds 31 and 184 desynced in some runs.
  - rlgenerals' new replay-determinism regression test failed in 18 of 20 runs before the fix, and passed in 10 of 10 runs with it.
  - The solo CRC of a seeded 3000-frame skirmish is unchanged.
